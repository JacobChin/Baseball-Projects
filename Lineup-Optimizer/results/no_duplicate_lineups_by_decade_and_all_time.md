## Model Scope

- Actual strikeouts and stolen bases are retained for every era.
- Actual caught stealing is used where recorded; missing caught-stealing values are standardized to a 75% success rate.
- Historical seasons through 2019 use common neutral Sprint Speed, home-to-first, and batted-ball detail. Seasons from 2020-2025 use the available modern inputs.
- Contextual walks are disabled for this cross-era common-input comparison.
- The decade and all-time finals use 100,000 simulated games per reported order.
- This is a broad staged simulation search, not an exhaustive proof over every possible player combination and all 9! batting orders.
